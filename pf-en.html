Security

It reads your numbers. Nothing else.

Closewise is handed finished exports, matches them and reports. It needs no more access than that.

Processed in the EU

Matching runs on servers in the EU (Ireland). Your data does not leave the EU.

Your ledger is not kept

Your night is read, matched and reported. What is kept is the exception record – what was flagged, where, and why – not the raw ledger.

It is handed exports

Closewise never writes to your PMS, your POS or your bank. A write is not possible rather than merely not done.
